Write the Word

Write the Word is an iPad app for copying Scripture by hand with Apple Pencil. It requires an iPad running iPadOS 17 or later, and an Apple Pencil to write.

Contact

Bug, question, or idea? Email ysuh@dotollabs.com. Please include your iPad model and iPadOS version, and what you were doing when the problem happened. I read every message and reply as soon as I can.

Common questions

Why can't I select a verse or write with my finger?

This is by design. In the reading area, only the Apple Pencil selects verses and writes; a finger only scrolls. That way resting your hand or scrolling can never change your work by accident. In the Library, Settings, and the toolbar, your finger works normally.

How do I start writing?

Tap the Library button (top left) to choose a book and chapter. Tap a verse with the Pencil to select it, then write on the ruled lines. Tap the Next Verse arrow, or just start writing on the next verse.

Will my writing sync to my other iPads?

Yes, through your own iCloud account. Both iPads need to be signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on for the app, and they need a network connection. Sync can take a minute or two. If something doesn't appear, check that your iCloud storage isn't full, then reopen the app on both iPads.

How do I back up my writing?

Open Settings and choose Back Up. When the share sheet appears, save the file to Files or iCloud Drive, not just the share sheet, so it survives even if the app is deleted. Restore brings a backup back on this or another iPad. Restore replaces everything currently in the app, so back up first if you're unsure.

How do I erase everything?

Settings → Erase All Notes & Images. This permanently deletes your notes, drawings, images, highlights, bookmarks, and handwritten verses, and it can't be undone. If iCloud sync is on, the deletion syncs to your other iPads too.

Why does Convert to Text only work for some translations?

Handwriting recognition currently reads English handwriting, so Convert to Text is offered for the English translations, not the Korean one.

My reminders don't appear.

Turn on Daily Reminder in Settings and allow notifications when asked. If you didn't see the prompt, notifications may be off for the app in iOS Settings → Notifications.
